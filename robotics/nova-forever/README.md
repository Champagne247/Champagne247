# NOVA Forever Build

**Status:** concept / pre-engineering (2026-10-26). No tested CAD, STL, electronics or physical prototypes are represented here.

Three distinct 10–12 inch target-scale companions share a model-agnostic architecture and a universal mechanical/electrical docking standard.

| Character | Identity | Color | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| NOVA | Navigational Observation & Versatile Autonomy | Orange / black | Exploration, perception, mapping, vehicle operation |
| MILO | Modular Intelligent Life Orchestrator | White / electric blue | Communication, routines, family assistance |
| ZIGGY | Working acronym TBD | Lime green / gunmetal | Tools, prototyping, diagnostics, maker education |

## Principles
- Each robot must be lovable, distinctive, functional, serviceable and buildable.
- Shared dock standard; separate personalities, body shells and mission modules.
- AI is portable and model-agnostic; no cloud service or chassis is a hard dependency.
- Design for printable parts, common fasteners, replaceable electronics and safe battery servicing.
- No autonomous high-energy tools, unguarded flight, or hazardous operation without dedicated safety engineering.

## Development sequence
1. Requirements and design freeze for a minimum viable prototype.
2. Bench-test power, compute, vision, voice, actuators and emergency stop.
3. Validate dock geometry with non-powered printed fit-test coupons.
4. Build a safe low-speed rolling/tracked prototype; walking is a later milestone.
5. Integrate expressive face and personality software.
6. Demonstrate repeatable docking and controlled power transfer.
7. Expand to three shells, tool carriers and eventually flight after separate safety review.
