# NOVA Family — Visual Design Lock v1.0
Date: 2026-10-26
Status: visual identity locked; mechanical dimensions and functionality NOT validated.

## Reference
User-provided NOVA FAMILY concept board (2026-10-26). The artwork is the approved aesthetic target, not dimensioned CAD or proof of hardware performance. Original image is in the conversation; binary asset is not yet committed to GitHub.

## Non-negotiable visual identity
- NOVA / explorer: vivid orange + black/gunmetal; rugged protective armor; oversized paired binocular/lens-like eyes; blue illuminated expression accents; compact sturdy silhouette.
- MILO / helper: white + electric blue; broad, smooth black digital faceplate; soft rounded shapes, glowing blue expressive eyes; friendly approachable proportions.
- ZIGGY / inventor: lime green + dark gunmetal; angular playful armor; prominent pointed antenna/ear structures; expressive green eyes; visible maker/tool styling.
- No two characters share the same dominant accent color or head silhouette.
- All are visually distinct, recognizable at thumbnail scale, expressive, repairable, and compatible with a common dock standard.

## Shared design language
- Target height 10–12 inches (subject to packaging/stability studies).
- Exposed mechanical joints with guards, replaceable armor panels, accessible fasteners and service hatches.
- Real cameras and audio integrated without sacrificing expressions; status indicators for active sensors.
- Common rear docking interface with keyed alignment, positive retention, and separate protected power/data interfaces.
- Vehicle modules: tracks, wheels, tool carrier, HOME station; aircraft only after separate engineering safety validation.
- The image shows idealized walking and dexterous hands: future targets, not first prototype features.

## Change control
Preserve head silhouette, dominant color, eye concept and character personality. Adjust interior geometry, part splits, vent placement, camera windows, lens/bezel design, motor mounts, proportions within reasonable bounds, and connector placement when required by measured components. Record visual deviations before release. Do not claim photo-accurate replication until CAD/physical validation.

## First manufacturing focus
NOVA B01 binocular head. First validate ordered ESP32-S3 Sense, dual SSD1309 OLED candidates, MG90S servos, PCA9685, TCA9548A, MAX98357A and speaker. Build a bench electronics mockup; then measure physical components and engineer printable enclosure. An OLED is not a camera lens; design bezel/optics deliberately.

## Acceptance
At concept review: three clearly distinct silhouettes/colors. At head prototype: two independently expressive eyes, safe head pan/tilt, audio and camera integration where supported, maintainable shell. At family platform: measured common dock fit and mechanical/electrical safety tests.
