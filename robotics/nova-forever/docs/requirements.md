# System requirements — draft v0.1

## Target experience
- Three visibly different characters: NOVA orange/black, MILO white/blue, ZIGGY green/gunmetal.
- Friendly expressive eyes/mouth and voice, with camera privacy indicators.
- Target overall height 10–12 inches; verify feasibility against motors, battery and stability.
- Independent basic locomotion; locomotion mechanism TBD by prototype test.
- Universal rear dock enabling secure connection to a home station and optional carrier.

## Measurable acceptance tests (proposed)
- Character boots and can enter safe idle without network access.
- Emergency stop disables actuator drive; recovery requires deliberate reset.
- Camera/microphone active indicators are visible and user-controlled.
- Battery, compute and actuator modules are serviceable without destroying the shell.
- Dock has keyed orientation, retention sensing and no exposed energized contacts while undocked.
- Dock disconnect under load enters a safe state.
- Prototype passes a supervised low-speed navigation demonstration.

## Open engineering decisions
Mass budget, center of gravity, biped versus wheeled prototype, exact motor torque, compute board, battery chemistry/capacity, power architecture, connector current rating, fastener system, manufacturing tolerances, water resistance, camera module, local speech stack, costs and sourcing.

All dimensions, prices and performance claims require measurements or vendor verification before release.
