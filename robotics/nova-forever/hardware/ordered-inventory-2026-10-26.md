## Procurement hold
Pause duplicate orders for displays, jumper wires, servos, audio and PWM boards until SKU reconciliation. Do not connect unidentified adapters to the servos or SBC. Capacitors require polarity and voltage checks. Breadboards are for low-current logic prototyping, not motor power distribution.

## Missing/unknown for full MVP
SBC or reusable phone interface, microcontroller, motor driver, drive motors, protected battery/charger, voltage regulators, hardwired E-stop, fuses, structural components, sensors and mounting hardware. Unknown does not mean not ordered; request remaining order pages and product-detail screenshots.

## Receiving checklist
Record model/SKU, quantity, condition, dimensions, electrical specifications, links, actual receipt date and photo of labels. Test one component at a time with current-limited supply and documented wiring.
