# CAD and printable-parts plan

Preferred editable source: FreeCAD or OpenSCAD; export STEP where practical plus 3MF/STL for printing. Never treat image concepts as existing manufacturing files.

## Part hierarchy
1. Shared NOVA Dock mechanical test coupon and mating gauge.
2. Common compute tray and protected battery tray with accessible fasteners.
3. Drive-base motor brackets, encoder cable routes and wheel/tracks guards.
4. NOVA head pan/tilt bracket and expressive face housing.
5. Removable orange/black torso, arms and service panels.
6. MILO and ZIGGY shells only after common dimensions are validated.

## CAD release checklist
Part number, version, units mm, source model, export, printer/material, orientation, clearance assumptions, assembly drawing, BOM link, print photo, fit test and revision notes. Measure connector, motor, screen and camera before defining cutouts. No final STL exists yet.
