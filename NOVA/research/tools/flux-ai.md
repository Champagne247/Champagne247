# Flux.ai — AI-assisted PCB design

- Website: https://www.flux.ai/
- Added: 2026-10-26
- Project: NOVA Forever Build
- Status: Research candidate; not yet evaluated or adopted
- Priority: High for future custom electronics design

## Why evaluate
Flux.ai advertises AI-assisted printed circuit board design. Explore whether it can speed up prototype schematic capture, component selection, PCB layout and review for NOVA's modular robotics architecture.

## Candidate applications
- NOVA, BUDDY and PIP compact sensor and control boards
- Universal NOVA Dock Interface: module identification, data and power connections, locking and safety interlocks
- HOME charging/service dock interface electronics
- Motor-driver and actuator interface boards
- Battery and power-distribution monitoring (with independent electrical and safety validation)

## Evaluation checklist
- Verify pricing, export formats, design ownership and collaboration features
- Check compatibility with KiCad, fabrication and assembly vendors
- Prototype a low-voltage sensor/interface board before attempting battery charging or motor-power circuitry
- Independently review schematics, electrical ratings, thermal limits, protection and PCB design rules before fabrication

This entry records a research lead, not a verified technical endorsement or finished design.
