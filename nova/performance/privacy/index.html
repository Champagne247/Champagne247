N.O.V.A. / PERFORMANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

NOVA Performance is Christopher Michael Champagne’s personal WHOOP integration for reviewing fitness, sleep, training load and recovery. This initial version is intended for the owner’s personal use.

Information accessed

With your authorization, the connector may access WHOOP profile information, body measurements, physiological cycles and strain, recovery scores, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep records and workout records. It also receives access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the connection.

How information is used

Information is used to display personal readiness and training trends and support personal performance planning. It is not used for advertising or sold. The initial connector does not send WHOOP information to an AI provider, Telegram, or the public M.A.P. feed. Adding those features will require an updated policy and an explicit choice to enable them.

Storage and access

The public GitHub Pages site contains this policy, not your WHOOP records or credentials. The connector is designed to store OAuth tokens encrypted on its private server. Authorized server operators and the hosting provider may have access to server infrastructure. The initial connector retrieves WHOOP records on request and does not retain a separate workout or health-data history. No server is connected until deployment and authorization are completed.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained while the integration is connected. Disconnecting through the connector revokes WHOOP access and deletes its stored token file. You can also revoke access through WHOOP and ask the operator to remove any remaining local credentials. Hosting-provider logs and backups, if enabled, follow that provider’s retention settings; the connector is designed not to log tokens or health records.

Your choices

You choose whether to authorize WHOOP access and can withdraw it. This service provides personal fitness information, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Operator and contact

Christopher Michael Champagne operates NOVA Performance. For privacy, access or deletion questions, contact the operator through his GitHub profile. Do not post health records or credentials in public issues.

Changes

This page will be updated before material changes to data use, sharing or storage.
