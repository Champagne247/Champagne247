NOVA Forever Build

In progress · Engineering and prototyping

From imagination to physical robotics. We're exploring a family of expressive, modular companion robots that can move independently and connect to compatible carriers and tools.

The vision

NOVA

Rugged explorer with expressive camera eyes.

BUDDY

Friendly, approachable helper.

PIP

Playful inventor and tinkerer.

Character designs and names are provisional. A shared universal docking interface is the long-term foundation.

Our engineering stack

Onshape for mechanical design, Hugging Face for evaluating AI models and robotics research, and GitHub for documentation and source control.

What's happening now

We're organizing the initial parts inventory, engineering requirements, receiving checklist and prototype build log. The first practical milestone is inspecting delivered hardware and designing a safe, affordable working prototype.

Real CAD, electronics and software will be added as they're created and tested. This is an experimental build, not a finished product.
