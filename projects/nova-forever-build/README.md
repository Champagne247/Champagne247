# NOVA Forever Build

Status: concept / engineering planning — October 26, 2026.

A modular, model-agnostic robotics ecosystem: expressive independent companion robots and interchangeable powered carriers, all designed around a reusable universal dock.

## Characters (provisional)
- NOVA: rugged orange/white explorer with binocular camera eyes.
- BUDDY: smooth white/blue helper with animated face.
- PIP: cream/orange inventor with antenna-like accents.

## First prototype
A small expressive companion with voice, camera eyes and a tracked carrier, with a shared dock standard. Design, simulate and validate before manufacturing.

## Toolchain
- Onshape: parametric CAD, assemblies, fabrication exports; verify simulation/manufacturing integrations and plan limits.
- Hugging Face: evaluate speech, vision and robotics models and datasets, with license and resource checks.
- GitHub: source control, issues, review and documentation.
- ROS 2 / simulator: evaluate when software and hardware integration warrants it.

## Build gates
1. Requirements and budget; confirm already ordered hardware.
2. Dimensioned universal dock specification, locking and safety interlocks.
3. Mechanical CAD and assembly fit checks.
4. Electronics power budget, battery protection and wiring design.
5. Prototype fabrication and bench testing.
6. Motion, voice and perception integration.
7. Repeatable manufacturing and test documentation.

No production CAD, tested STL, firmware or verified supplier order is claimed yet. Do not publish personal information, credentials or proprietary files without review.
