## NOVA Forever Build — Robotics & Innovation

At CC's Technical Services, our work in electrical systems, security, low voltage and technical problem-solving is inspiring a new hands-on research and development project: NOVA Forever Build.

We're exploring affordable, expressive modular robots that combine 3D-designed mechanical systems, electronics, cameras, voice and open-source software. The vision is a family of friendly robots that can operate independently and connect to interchangeable carriers and tools through a shared docking interface.

**Current phase:** planning, parts evaluation and prototype engineering. This is an experimental project, not an available product or service.

Follow our progress as we document designs, fabrication, assembly and real-world testing.

Suggested links: project GitHub repository (once established), M.A.P. project journal, photos and videos (once authorized).
