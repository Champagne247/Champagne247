## My M.A.P.

**M.A.P. — Mission Alignment Protocol**

M.A.P. transforms dreams, goals and real-life experiences into a visible, connected journey.

One Master Life Entry can connect to:

- Multiple life categories
- Timeline entries
- Interactive map locations
- Photo and video galleries
- Projects and opportunities
- External links
- Social publishing destinations
- Future N.O.V.A. automation
