FROM AN IDEA TO SOMETHING YOU CAN PLAY

V2 is here. Come hunt us down.

We asked a simple question: can we build a fun first-person shooter that friends can play together on their phones?

Meet CROSSFIRE: Pocket Arena. Two 3D arenas, three weapons, selectable crosshairs, and more room for your rivals. Choose eight-player free-for-all or 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, and 4v4 teams. Browse public rooms or create your own.

Up to 8 playersFree-for-all + teams2 maps3 weaponsCustom crosshairsNo downloads

This is a working experiment, and you’re invited to help shape it. V2 capacity and gameplay checks pass for all five modes, and separate browser sessions have connected. Eight physical phones and full WebGL graphics have not yet been tested together. Expect rough edges.

Open the arena & play ↗

Enter your friend’s six-character code. On the arena screen, enter your name and tap Join.

No code yet? Open the arena and create a room. You can also browse active public rooms directly in the game—no code needed to find a match.

THE PLAN IS SIMPLE

Bring your friends. Find your rivals.

  1. 01

    Drop in.

    Open the arena, choose your callsign, and tap Create a Room. Playing alone? Try the solo firing range first.

  2. 02

    Fill the room.

    Have up to seven friends visit this page. Join from the public room browser or enter the room code. Team modes automatically assign cyan and coral teams.

  3. 03

    Run it back.

    Use cover, watch your ammo, and chase the top score. Eliminated? You’ll automatically respawn after three seconds.

On your phone

Turn sideways for the best view. Hold the left pad to move. Drag the right side to aim. Hold FIRE to shoot and tap RELOAD for a fresh magazine. AIM toggles the sight view; SWITCH cycles weapons. Open the menu for the armory and crosshair options.

On your computer

WASD to move. Click the arena for mouse look. Hold the left mouse button to fire. R reloads; 1, 2, and 3 switch guns. Esc releases your mouse.
