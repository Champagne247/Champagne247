FROM AN IDEA TO SOMETHING YOU CAN PLAY

Come hunt us down.

We asked a simple question: can we build a fun little first-person shooter that four people can play together on their phones?

Meet CROSSFIRE: Pocket Arena. A small map, one assault rifle, cover to work around, and your friends on the other side. Move, aim, fire, reload—and come back for the rematch.

Up to 4 playersOnline free-for-allTouch controlsAutomatic respawnsNo downloads

This is a working experiment, and you’re invited to help shape it. Four browser sessions have connected together; real-phone controls and connection quality are still being tested. Expect rough edges.

Open the arena & play ↗

Enter your friend’s six-character code. On the arena screen, enter your name and tap Join.

No code yet? Open the arena and create a room. This is room-based play; there’s no public matchmaking list.

THE PLAN IS SIMPLE

Bring your friends. Find your rivals.

  1. 01

    Drop in.

    Open the arena, choose your callsign, and tap Create a Room. Playing alone? Try the solo firing range first.

  2. 02

    Fill the room.

    Have up to three friends visit this page and enter your room code. You can each play from your own phone.

  3. 03

    Run it back.

    Use cover, watch your ammo, and chase the top score. Eliminated? You’ll automatically respawn after three seconds.

On your phone

Turn sideways for the best view. Hold the left pad to move. Drag the right side to aim. Hold FIRE to shoot and tap RELOAD for a fresh magazine.

On your computer

WASD to move. Click the arena for mouse look. Hold the left mouse button to fire. R reloads; Esc releases your mouse.
